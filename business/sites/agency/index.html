The starting point

Common website problems local businesses run into

Most of these are simply unfinished work, not failures. Each one has a straightforward fix — and that is the whole point of this studio.

  • No website at all

    Customers who search for you find a bare directory listing or a competitor instead. A single well-structured page can close that gap.

  • Outdated design

    An old layout, broken links or stale opening hours make a good business look inactive. A refresh restores confidence quickly.

  • Not mobile-friendly

    Most local searches happen on a phone. When text is tiny or buttons are hard to tap, visitors leave before they ever contact you.

  • Slow or hard to update

    Heavy pages and clunky editors make small changes feel like a project. Lean static pages load fast and stay easy to edit.

Process

Six steps from brief to launch

Typical turnaround 2-5 days for a starter site. Larger scopes are scheduled once the brief is confirmed — that is an estimate, not a guarantee.

  1. 01

    Brief

    A short message or call about your business, your goals, must-have features and any branding you already have.

  2. 02

    Research

    I review your local market, what competitors are doing and the phrases customers actually search for.

  3. 03

    Design

    Structure and visual direction: layout, colours, typography and a copy outline, approved by you before build.

  4. 04

    Build

    Mobile-first coding, forms and integrations. You get a preview link while the site takes shape.

  5. 05

    QA

    Responsive checks across screen sizes, accessibility checks, link and form testing, and load-time cleanup.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Deployment, domain connection, optional analytics with your authorization, and full handover of files and access.

FAQ

Questions people ask before starting

Who owns the website when the project is done?

You do. After final payment you receive the site files, assets and the access needed to run them. Nothing is locked to me, and you are free to move hosting or work with someone else later.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included on design and build. If you need more, I quote the extra work before continuing so the scope and price stay agreed.

What content do you need from me?

Text for each page, your logo and brand colours if you have them, photos (or I use honest placeholder areas until you have them), contact details, and any booking or map links. A short questionnaire covers all of this at the brief stage.

Do you handle hosting?

Yes. I can deploy to an account you already own or set one up for you and explain the running costs plainly. Hosting management and small updates are also what the $25/month maintenance plan covers.

How do payments work?

50% upfront to start the project and 50% before handover. Prices in USD; final quote confirmed per project.

What does the maintenance plan include?

$25/month covers hosting upkeep, small content edits, minor fixes and support. Larger changes such as new pages or new features are quoted separately before any work begins.
