Services

Everything the family needs, under one roof

Six core treatments, described plainly. Every plan is discussed and priced with you first.

  • Checkups

    A full examination of teeth, gums and bite, with photographs or X-rays only when they are needed.

  • Professional cleaning

    Scaling and polish to remove hardened plaque, followed by practical home-care advice.

  • Teeth whitening

    In-clinic or take-home whitening, with shade guidance and an honest note on what results to expect.

  • Veneers

    Thin front-surface shells for shape, colour and spacing, always previewed with a trial smile first.

  • Invisible aligners

    Clear, removable trays planned from a digital scan, with a review visit every few weeks.

  • Emergency care

    Same-day assessment for toothache, swelling, knocked-out teeth and lost fillings or crowns.

How we work

Process you can watch, not claims you have to trust

These are the routine steps built into every appointment at this (fictional) practice.

  • Sterilization protocol

    Every instrument set is cleaned, packaged and run through an autoclave; pouch indicators are checked before a pack is opened in front of you.

  • Digital X-rays

    Sensor-based images replace film, appear on the chair-side monitor within seconds, and can be enlarged for you to see what we see.

  • Same-day emergency slots

    Two appointment slots each morning and afternoon are held open for urgent problems, so toothache does not wait a week.

  • Costs agreed up front

    A written estimate listing each item and its fee is handed over before treatment starts; nothing extra is added without asking you first.

FAQ

Common questions

What happens at my first visit?

Allow about 45 minutes: we review your medical history, examine your teeth and gums, take X-rays only if needed, then talk through any findings and agree on next steps. Nothing is treated on the same day unless you ask us to and it is straightforward.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Bring your policy details to your appointment and we will prepare the paperwork your insurer asks for, including itemised invoices. Coverage varies by plan, so we cannot guarantee reimbursement amounts — your insurer makes that decision.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call the practice phone number straight away; two slots each morning and afternoon are held for urgent cases. For a knocked-out adult tooth, keep it in milk and see us immediately. If we are closed, use your local emergency dental service.

What payment options are available?

Payment is taken at the end of each appointment by card or bank transfer. For larger treatments you can split the cost across scheduled visits, and a written estimate is always provided before work begins.
